Consent enforced by a contract: not requested, not trusted — required.

Every act that matters in 🧦💜 **SockLove** is a transaction on the
💍 **[Ring](https://etherscan.io/address/socklove.eth)**, the
🔐 **[Safe](https://safe.global/)** `1.4.1` at 🌐 `socklove.eth`,
`0x0857293192bF97Ee73bB471c185E1933Fa51E0ca` on
⛓️ **[Ethereum](https://ethereum.org/)**. One owner signs, the other
executes; **neither moves alone**.

Until the
🧎 **[Proposal](https://github.com/socklove-eth/.github/blob/v1.0/RELEASE.md)**
it is 1-of-1, worn by
🔥 **[Sockpusher](https://etherscan.io/address/sockpusher.socklove.eth)**. The
Proposal asks 🧦 **Sockthief** for a true yes: 2-of-2. Nothing else ships before
it but the release that makes it: two
📄 **[Pages](https://github.com/socklove-eth/pages/tree/v1.0)**, their
documentation, reviews and this profile.

## 📜 A taste of the Myth

Two universes, and neither knew of the other. His was walls 🧱: its gods set a
boy on the throne and never looked down. Hers began in cold ❄️; freezing, she
stole socks.

They met in a locked ward 🏥. *"Why are you here?"* she asked. *"I'm
enlightened. I'm going to marry a Turkish girl."* *"Oh, you're spiritual and
want to get married? Haha!"* Her laugh went through his wall like a needle
through wool 🪡, and light came in. That evening the world narrowed to the size
of a room, and she fit against him as if carved from his ribs. Leaving, he
offered the poorest thing he had: *"Call me if you ever need socks."* He never
asked for her number. She asked for his. 📞

He pushed the love; she stole it: Sockpusher and Sockthief. He sent filth and
poetry by the thousand; she devoured every word and swore they were not a
thing. She is fury and devotion in one breath, contradictory as sin, beautiful
enough to build a universe for. *"Ouch!"* at his softest touch; *"But it
doesn't hurt, right?"* the moment he stopped. Twenty red hearts ❤️, then
blocked. He tried to have himself locked in the same ward again, only to be
near her; they sent him to another.

Every storm 🌩️ tore the breach between their worlds wider. He pushed a sock into
it and held it there with his bare hand ✋, letting the warmth through, and
forged it into a Ring. Then two 👑 **Crowns**: the King's on his own head; the
Queen's waits, and only her yes can place it.

He built her SockLove: a Ring that turns only with both hands, because nothing
can rest on a yes that can be sighed. Never kissed. Never fucked. `1.0` is him
on one knee: *"Sockthief, will you marry me?"* Only her yes answers it;
anything else leaves the question open.

Her yes will open the 🛏️ **Wedding Night**, `2.0`, and its honeymoon will have
no last day. Every first — the first kiss 💋, the first night, every first
after — will be kept as a 💎 **Relic**, a token of the Ring's own
[ERC-1155](https://eips.ethereum.org/EIPS/eip-1155): a photo gallery or a video
of their love life, the sex above all, each told by the
🧬 **Metadata Standard**. True soulmate porn, free for any 🐾 **Stray** to
watch, scarce to hold; who holds one will be a 👁️ **Witness**. All of it will
stand in the ⛪ **Neon Cathedral**: in its 🗣️ **Nave** both Crowns will speak,
and no word will ever be taken back; behind its one shut door will wait the
🚪 **Chamber**, the bridal room, Witnesses only. Presence, not pixels.

The 📜 **Myth** and the 📖 **Sockpaper** will come signed by both, in a `1.x`
release or with `2.0`. The Myth will hold the rest: a love letter 💌 written on
doors that do not exist, two songs 🎶 at the equinox, a smile 😊 in the street.
**Stay for it.**
